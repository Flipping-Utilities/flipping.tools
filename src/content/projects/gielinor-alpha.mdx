## Overview

Gielinor Alpha is an independent credit marketplace for ranked Old School RuneScape Grand Exchange opportunities. It is not affiliated with Jagex. Listings show return, expected profit, capital, confidence, and timing so you can compare hunts, while the item name and GP prices stay hidden until you unlock an allocation. Unlocks are advice only — you place every trade yourself. Nothing is auto-executed in-game.

## Features

- **Masked opportunity board** - Compare ranked GE listings without seeing the item until purchase
- **Recommendations** - Opportunities ranked from public GE price and volume data, then published as listings
- **Buy Now and bids** - Spend credits to unlock an allocation, or bid against other traders on the same listing
- **Daily free credits** - Claim a daily grant so you can try the desk without paying first
- **Manual GE placement** - No bot, sniper, or in-game automation

## Pricing

Freemium. Daily free credits are available to try unlocks. Extra credits can be purchased. Credits have no cash value and cannot be cashed out.
